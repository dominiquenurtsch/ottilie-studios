Dominique (@dom.overseas) is an Australian luxury fashion, beauty and travel content creator known for her refined minimalist aesthetic and globally engaged audience.

Her signature elevated photo and video content has not only gone viral across multiple platforms but has also been adored and published by Australian and global brands since 2009. Canva, Tory Burch, H&M, and Pandora are part of her extensive client portfolio.

Her focus lies in creating captivating content, both within Australia and overseas during her frequent travels to Europe. She travels bi-annually to Paris Fashion Week, producing editorial content for both luxury and high-street brands. Her work spans fashion, beauty, travel and lifestyle, blending effortless style with modern storytelling to create high-impact digital content, UGC and premium campaign assets.

Dominique partners with luxury hotels, global fashion houses and beauty brands to deliver highly converting visuals, destination storytelling and elevated brand narratives that resonate strongly with audiences across Australia, the United States and Europe.

She is available for bespoke UGC, event attendance and aligned paid social partnerships.
